The First 72 Hours.

In every major disaster of the last decade—from Fukushima to Puerto Rico—the primary cause of preventable death wasn't the event itself. It was the information blackout that followed.

01.

Centralized = Vulnerable

Modern communication is a "Star Topology." Your phone talks to a tower. If the tower loses power (or snaps in a storm), you lose everything. It is a single point of failure designed for profit, not survival.

02.

The "Last Mile" Gap

Satellites (Starlink) are amazing, but they are expensive, power-hungry, and fragile. You can't put a satellite dish in every backpack. We need a local, terrestrial layer that works in the mud.

Infrastructure in a Box.

We built Aegis to be the "Digital Lifeboat" for communities. It connects people to people, independent of the global internet.

  • ✓ Solar Autonomous: Runs for years without maintenance.
  • ✓ Long Range: 15km+ utilizing LoRa modulation.
  • ✓ Zero Config: Turn it on. It finds the mesh.
