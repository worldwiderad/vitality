What is the World Scholar's Cup?

The World Scholar's Cup (WSC) is more than just a competition; it is a celebration of learning. It brings together top scholars from around the globe to demonstrate their skills, connect with other students, and engage in critical thinking.

It is widely considered one of the most enjoyable extracurricular activities available, blending academic rigor with social events like cultural fairs, scavenger hunts, and the famous Scholar's Ball. You will compete in teams of three, exploring a specific theme that changes every year, covering subjects ranging from Science and History to Literature and The Arts.

The Experience

Every round consists of four main events that challenge different skill sets:

Team Debate

Collaborate with your team to argue persuasive motions on global issues. You'll debate three times per round!

Collaborative Writing

Debate with your pen. You'll be given six prompts, and each teammate must pick one to write a persuasive essay on.

Scholar's Bowl

A fast-paced, loud, and multimedia team quiz where you race against the clock (and other teams) to solve analytical questions.

Scholar's Challenge

A multiple-choice exam that looks like a test but feels like a puzzle, allowing you to select multiple answers if you aren't sure.

The Journey to Yale

Your season begins here at CIS, but it can take you around the world.

  1. The Regional Round (May): Compete right here in Singapore. Top teams qualify for the Global Round.
  2. The Global Round (Summer): Travel with the CIS team! In previous years, we have traveled to Bangkok to compete against thousands of scholars from dozens of countries.
  3. The Tournament of Champions (November): The best of the best are invited to the final round hosted on the historic campus of Yale University in New Haven, USA.
